Q1 / Locked

Scope

Japanese only, kanji delayed until after the kana phase. Other languages keep their current flow. Generalize later if it pays.

Q2 / Locked

Kanji-delay strategy

Pure kana phase first, then a kanji ramp. Words exist in their kana form throughout the early curriculum. Once kana is mastered, kanji is introduced gradually and the same annotation primitive switches from romaji-over-kana to kana-over-kanji.

Q3 / Locked

Lesson granularity

Row-spine curriculum + anchor words allowed regardless of kana coverage. Lessons step through canonical kana rows. Real-meaning anchor words appear from day 1 even when they contain kana not yet introduced — the renderer handles it via romaji helpers above unfamiliar kana.

Q4 / Locked

Helper behavior

Auto-fade per kana, conservative threshold. No learner-facing toggle. A helper hides only when the kana has crossed a generous bar: 20 exposures AND SRS interval ≥ 7 days (AND-gate, not OR). Five drills yesterday isn't enough.

Q5 / Locked

Reach of the annotation primitive

Everywhere Japanese text appears. One <AnnotatedJa> component used in lesson steps + flashcard previews (Phase 1) and stories (Phase 2). Bare-string mode handles kana-only text with zero per-word metadata.

Q6 / Locked

Relationship to the standalone alphabet learner

Lessons contain everything; alphabet learner stays as free-practice. Each course lesson includes its own kana-teaching steps inline with word drills. The existing learner at /practice/alphabet/hiragana/learn is untouched (no conflict with the parallel agent in there).

Q7 / Locked

Phasing

Vertical slice — a-row only, end-to-end. Phase 1 builds every layer (renderer, mastery state, data model, content, integration, backend sync) for just the a-row + first words. Other rows + auto-generator come later. Most de-risking; clearest milestone.
