# KO gap audit — taught coverage vs the graded/frequency list (2026-08-26)

**Data:** `docs/data/ko-graded-vocab.json` (5,795 entries; NIKL 학습용 어휘 목록
2003 spine + **homograph-aware** 2002 corpus ranks + 국제통용 2017 levels —
generated by `node scripts/ingest-ko-frequency.mjs --graded-vocab`; raw-file
provenance in `scripts/data/ko-frequency-raw.SOURCES.md`).
**Taught set:** 333 distinct surfaces from `ko/courseAtoms.ts`
(vocab/particle/phrase kinds with a real `fromModule`), measured against the
2026-08-26 working tree (includes the uncommitted Tier-1 audit edits).

**Method:** surface-level match, plus two adjustments: a taught `N하다` covers
`N` (per research doc §4.4), and a taught polite present form covers its
dictionary-form predicate (있어요 covers 있다 — the KO course teaches 해요체
forms, not dict forms; simple contraction rules, so ㅂ/르-irregulars may still
be under-counted by a word or two). Ranks are homograph-aware, so these
numbers are NOT comparable to the §2 table in
`docs/ko-freq-level-research-2026-08-26.md` (that one was measured on the
smeared ranks: 500 slots ≈ 389 distinct surfaces; here 500 slots = 500 words).
**Register caveat:** the 2002 ranks are written-corpus; KO still has no spoken
signal (blocked on the Modu agreement).

Reading: the course has touched about a third of the level-1/grade-A core and
almost nothing beyond it. 742 grade-A-or-level-1 words with a corpus rank are
untaught. The top-100 holes below are the highest-value word list KO can get
today; the interleave + insert-lesson mechanism from the JA wave applies.

Notes for the re-author, before treating every row as a hole:
- **Predicate holes are dict-form lemmas** (되다, 않다, 알다, 그렇다…). Several
  are function-like (않다, 말다, 싶다-family) — they are *grammar-lesson*
  targets, not vocab-card targets; the intl list agrees (it files them under
  its grammar sheet, hence `intlLevel: null` on some).
- 씨 (Mr./Ms.), 수 (way/means), 것/거 are structure words — teach through
  grammar points, not image MCQs.
- Written-register words (그러나 #30) should NOT jump the queue over their
  spoken forms (근데/그런데) — check register before authoring.
