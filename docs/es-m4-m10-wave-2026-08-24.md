## Laws (short form — the guide is binding, this is the checklist)

1. IR shape: `frame: none`, `expectedLessonCount: 10`, `checkpoint: 8`,
   free lessons only, mastery ENDS on a sim. Kinds available: info,
   imageMcq, audioWimcq, sim, map, buildLit, listenCompLit,
   listenBuildLit, clozeLit, matchLit, speakLit(+cue: recall), textMcq,
   mcq. (m6 additionally gets agreementLit + genderSort — see its spec.)
2. Debuts: imageable → imageMcq (articled surface: «la mesa» never
   «mesa»); non-imageable → self-cueing micro-sim or map-elimination
   (max ONE new word per map); pretest never. Emoji must be HONEST
   (meaning, not sound-pun). Nouns wear articles in every surface; atoms
   stay bare with `gender`.
3. Sequence lints: no two adjacent same-type steps; never 4 consecutive
   selection steps (mcq/wimcq/cloze/listening-comp/agreement); teaching
   lessons 10–25 steps, cp 12–22 all-graded; match in the closing zone;
   every lesson ends on its OWN win (mastery on the sim); info ≤1 per
   teaching lesson, 0 in cp/mastery; cloze share ≤25% per lesson.
4. §13.9: review tail in every lesson (2–3 off-topic prior retrievals,
   ≥60% of lessons referencing prior modules); discrimination lanes
   ALTERNATE with both halves live and balanced counts; recall law —
   `cue: recall` NEVER before a printed voicing (cross-module licensed;
   each spec lists what it must voice printed); interleave repetitive
   families 3/3/2-style with unrelated breaks; ≥6 audio-prompted wimcqs;
   digit-option MCQs are audio-prompted; NO typed translate; sims
   self-cueing (mirror-trap, goal ≤8 words, debut = single correct,
   max-acceptance on tile banks); map→build is TRANSFER (different noun)
   or well-spaced — never the same sentence at i+2 (m3 confirmation-walk
   law).
5. Header carries a VOICING LEDGER + provenance notes like m3's.
6. Titles are promises; L1 states the module promise; win explanations
   stay short; glosses natural English ("a sheet of paper", never
   "is of Ana" without the bridge note).
7. Placement: 1 screener + ≥3 byModule, 3 distractors each, none echoing
   the answer.

Recall licenses (printed voicings that already exist): m1 — hola, adiós,

## m4 — "¿Dónde está?" (where things are)

Atoms (12): está (verb, "is (somewhere)"), dónde (other, "where",
hint: DON-deh), allí (other, "there", hint: ll = y, like llave: a-YEE),
los (particle, "the (el-words, plural)"), las (particle), mesa (f, NO
emoji — non-imageable debut), cama (f 🛏️), baño (m 🚽, hint: ny sound
from ñ: BA-nyo), cocina (f, 🍳, hint: soft c, like cero: ko-SEE-na),
agua (f 💧, hint: "feminine — but singular pairs with EL: el agua"),
cuarto (m, "room", no emoji — map/sim debut), foto (f 📷, hint: "-o but
a la-word — short for fotografía").
- Contrast card: «hay» says something EXISTS; «está» says WHERE it is.
  «hay una llave» / «la llave está en la mesa».
- The plural rule STATED (callback to m3's noticed -s): -s plus los/las.
  Counting payoff: ocho/nueve/diez finally used («hay nueve llaves»,
  «los libros están…» is BANNED — no están in m4, plurals ride hay).
- Discrimination lanes: está/es clozes (both live, alternating);
  aquí/allí by EAR; los/las alternating; el/la vs los/las transfers.
  agua exception beat: card + trials («el agua» but «es… fría»? NO
  adjectives yet — keep to el agua está en la cocina).
- foto & the secret-side lane: foto is -o but la — the m3 "secret side"
  card pays off ON a rule-breaker.
- Arc: L1 hay-vs-está + mesa/cama debuts + promise ("ask where anything
  is — and answer"); L2 rooms (baño/cocina/cuarto) + ¿dónde está? sims
  (Ana lost her keys AGAIN — the m3 gag continues); L3 allí + aquí/allí
  ear lane; L4 plural rule + los/las + big-number counting; L5 agua +
  foto (the two exceptions) ; L6 integration — the lost-celular hunt;
  L7 zero-new count-and-locate workout; L8 cp; L9 summit (helping Diego
  turn the house over before his date?); L10 mastery ends: Diego CALLS
  📞 asking where his things are.
- REQUIRED printed voicings: «¿dónde está?», «está en la mesa», «la
  cocina», «el agua», «allí», «la foto».

## m5 — "Mi familia" (have & belong)

Atoms (16): tengo, tienes, tiene (verbs, formulas — NEVER present the
paradigm), mi, tu (particles, hint on tu: "no accent — tú with the
accent is 'you'"), familia (f 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦), madre (f), padre (m), hermano (m 👦),
hermana (f 👧), abuelo (m 👴), abuela (f 👵), perro (m 🐶), gato (m 🐱),
años (m, "years"), ¿cuántos? (phrase, "how many?", KWAN-tos).
madre/padre have no honest emoji — sim/map debuts.
- Contrast: having & my/your. tener via three formulas.
- Lanes: tengo/tienes alternating; mi/tu alternating; hermano/hermana +
  abuelo/abuela by EAR (the -o/-a flip on PEOPLE again — bridge to m6).
- Beats: «¿cuántos años tienes?» → «tengo … años» (numbers payoff, any
  0–10); «¿tienes un perro?» sims (max-acceptance: sí/no + full forms);
  «mi madre se llama…» NO (se llama unlicensed) — use «mi madre es Ana»
  (es + name, m2-licensed).
- Interleave: family split 3/3/2 across L1–L5 with pets + tails as
  breaks; never a lesson that is only relatives.
- Arc: L1 tengo/mi + perro/gato debuts (promise: "talk about your
  people"); L2 madre/padre + tienes; L3 hermano/hermana + the ear lane;
  L4 abuelos + años/cuántos; L5 familia + foto payoff («la foto de mi

  L7 zero-new workout; L8 cp; L9 summit (Diego meets your dog at the

- REQUIRED printed voicings: «tengo un perro», «mi familia», «mi madre»,

## m6 — "Describe it" (agreement — the §13.4 payoff)

Atoms (16): grande, pequeño (hint: peh-KEH-nyo), bonito, nuevo, viejo,
bueno, malo (adjectives, register MASCULINE — the barGuards gender canon
derives feminines), muy, pero (particles), rojo, azul, verde, negro,
blanco (adjectives), carro (m 🚗), casa/libro etc. reused freely.
- Contrast: the sides REACH the describing words — bonito flips to
  bonita after la-words; azul/grande/verde don't flip (no -o). THE
  agreement callback card ("you've watched pink and blue for three
  modules — here's what they do").
- NEW STEP KINDS this module ships (emitters exist by the time it
  compiles): `agreementLit` (the agreement_cloze factory — «L__ cas__
  bonit__» style, blanks agree together) and `genderSort` (sort nouns
  onto their sides — the warm-up game, ≤2 per module). Both come OFF
  UNUSED_STEP_TYPES when this lands.
- Word order beat: adjective AFTER the noun — word_map CROSSING teaches
  it («el carro rojo» = "the red car" crossed), build produces it on a
  DIFFERENT noun.
- Lanes: bonito/bonita etc. alternating with both live (the
  buenos/buenas mechanism, now on adjectives); azul-type invariants
  explicitly trialed (the trap: adding -a to azul); negro/blanco +
  rojo/verde by ear w/ color swatches (🔴🔵🟢⚫⚪ ARE honest art).
- Tints: word_maps tint agreement CHAINS (la casa … bonita all pink).
- Arc: L1 the callback card + grande/pequeño; L2 colors I (rojo, azul,
  negro) interleaved with muy; L3 the flip (bonito/bonita) + agreement
  clozes; L4 colors II (verde, blanco) + carro + «el carro rojo» order;
  L5 nuevo/viejo + bueno/malo + pero («es viejo pero bonito» — the
  first compound thought!); L6 integration: the describing game (m3's
  naming game, level 2 — «¿qué es? — es una casa. ¿grande o pequeña?»);
  L7 zero-new workout; L8 cp; L9 summit: María's carro; L10 mastery
  ends on sim.
- REQUIRED printed voicings: «el carro rojo», «la casa es grande», «muy
  bonito», «es viejo pero bueno», «el carro es de María».
