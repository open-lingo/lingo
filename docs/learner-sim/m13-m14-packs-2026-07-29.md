## Findings

### Finding 1 — MAJOR: 4 of 8 new words in `ja-m13-neo-10` get zero recognition-only rep, ever

**Where:** おきる, あらう, ゆうべ, はやい — across their ENTIRE course life
(`ja-m13-neo-10` teaching lesson + `ja-m13-neo-review-3`, the pack's own
dedicated review). Every single graded touch of each of these four words,
first to last, is a production step:

- おきる: `ja-m13-neo-10-s-0` (build, as おきた), `-s-9` (translate, as
  おきたい), `ja-m13-neo-review-3-s-14` (build, dict form). Zero recognition.
- あらう: `ja-m13-neo-10-s-1` (build, dict form), `-challenge` (build, as
  あらった), `ja-m13-neo-review-3-s-19` (build, dict form). Zero recognition.
- ゆうべ: `ja-m13-neo-10-s-3` (listening_build — audio-reconstruction,
  production), `ja-m13-neo-review-3-s-17` (listening_build, production).
  Two touches total, both production.
- はやい (resolved correctly to `hayai-early`): `ja-m13-neo-10-s-6`
  (listening_build, production), `ja-m13-neo-review-3-s-16` (build,
  production). Two touches total, both production.

By contrast the other four pack words are properly scaffolded: みがく,
せっけん and シャワー are `imageable: true` and correctly debut on a
`word_image_mcq` (invariant 30 — `ja-m13-neo-10-debut-0/1/2`), and せっけん/
シャワー additionally get a `listening_comprehension` recognition check
in-module; けさ gets a genuine recognition rep via the `particle_cloze` at
`ja-m13-neo-10-cloze-10` (3-option pick, correctly modeled on the antiPattern
の-time contrast).

**Root cause:** おきる/あらう/ゆうべ/はやい are all `imageable: false`
(legitimately — no glyph can honestly show "this morning" or "get up"), so
they don't qualify for the invariant-30 image-MCQ debut, and unlike せっけん/
シャワー/けさ, this module gives them no SUBSTITUTE recognition check either.
This is the identical root cause and shape as the m11 weekday-lesson finding
(`docs/learner-sim/m11-packs-2026-07-29.md`, finding 2: "6 of 7 new weekday
words get no recognition-only rep") — an `imageable:false` new word with no
image-MCQ debut currently has no guaranteed recognition-first path in this
compiler, and nothing catches it (the guard `requireImageFirst` only fires
for imageable words).

**Fairness note:** softened by the fact that most of these are
tile-scaffolded builds (the learner picks a legible tile, not spelling cold)
and that R3 gives each word a second production touch spaced days later —
but a learner asked cold "what does ゆうべ mean" would have had zero
opportunity anywhere in the course to answer that in the cheaper,
lower-stakes direction (recognize→meaning) before being asked to produce it
in a full sentence.

### Finding 2 — MAJOR: あける and しめる — the pack's own rule-card-paired headline verbs — get zero recognition-only rep anywhere in the course

**Where:** `ja-m14-neo-10` and `ja-m14-neo-review-3` combined. Full
exposure list for each:

- あける: `ja-m14-neo-10-s-1` (build, as あけても), `-s-5` (build, as
  あけては), `-s-8` (build, as あけないで), `ja-m14-neo-review-3-s-14`
  (build, dict form). **Four touches, all production.** (The dictionary
  form あける never appears in a graded exercise until R3 — its only earlier
  appearance is inside the rule card's own prose/examples, which is pinned
  and ungraded.)
- しめる: `ja-m14-neo-10-s-0` (build, as しめて), `-s-4` (listening_build,
  dict form), `ja-m14-neo-review-3-s-13` (listening_build, dict form). Three
  touches, all production.

でんき is a milder instance of the same pattern: `ja-m14-neo-10-s-7` (build,
production) is its only touch in the teaching lesson; it gets exactly one
recognition rep, but only in the review lesson three lessons later
(`ja-m14-neo-review-3-lc-18`, listening_comprehension), not inside the
teaching lesson itself where a learner would want it.

By contrast もつ, ひく and つめたい (the module's three `imageable: true`
pack words) are correctly image-first (`ja-m14-neo-10-debut-0/1/2`), and もつ
additionally gets a real recognition rep in R3
(`ja-m14-neo-review-3-lc-15`, listening_comprehension). ドア gets one
recognition touch inside the teaching lesson itself
(`ja-m14-neo-10-lc-12`, listening_comprehension, "もう ドアを しめた").

**Severity note — why this is sharper than the m13 finding:** あける and
しめる are not incidental words in this pack; they are the two words the
whole lesson's rule card is built around ("あける opens a thing and しめる
closes it — a pair"), the ones a learner is most likely to be quizzed on
outside the app, and per the module's own IR notes they deliberately forfeit
their weak emoji (🔓) *specifically so they debut together on the rule card*
— a design choice made in full knowledge they'd get no image-MCQ. The
tradeoff (rule-card pairing over individual image debuts) is defensible, but
having made that tradeoff, the module still owes them *some* recognition
check (an MCQ, a cloze, a match-pair) before or during production, the same
way けさ got one in m13. Neither gets one anywhere.

**Fairness note:** every production ask is tile-scaffolded (not
free-typing), and R3 catches both words in dictionary form for the SRS
write, so the gap is about the LEARNING EXPERIENCE (never a cheap way to
check "do I recognize あける") rather than about SRS bookkeeping, which is
unaffected.

## What the learner actually experiences

**`ja-m13-neo-10`:** the rule card's mnemonic framing (けさ/ゆうべ as
relative-time siblings of きょう/きのう, both instantly familiar from
lesson 3) makes the new grammar land immediately, and the three imageable
words (みがく/せっけん/シャワー) get the textbook-correct picture-first
treatment. But the four abstract words carry the whole lesson's production
load with no soft landing: a learner who has just read "おきる is to get up"
once, in prose, is next asked to reconstruct "けさ しちじに おきた" from
tiles — workable (the tiles are legible and there's only one correct order),
but there's no moment anywhere before or after where they're simply asked
"which of these three means 'this morning'?" for おきる, あらう, ゆうべ, or
はやい the way けさ gets via the closing particle-cloze. It's not confusing,
just uniformly higher-stakes for those four words than the course's own
practice (image-first, or at minimum one recognition check) suggests it
should be.

**`ja-m13-neo-review-3`:** a fair, well-paced consolidation that correctly
reuses the character canon and mixes the module's whole grammar inventory
(たい/ほしい/すき/きらい) with the pack's words. It repeats the same
gap rather than fixing it — the four weak words get one more production
touch, not a recognition one — but that is consistent with its stated job
(the Track-A write surface), not a new failure.

**`ja-m14-neo-10`:** genuinely well-constructed grammar — the あける/しめる
pairing lands cleanly against the already-familiar て+ください/てもいい/
てはいけません/ないでください family, and もっている is a satisfying "oh,
same pattern as しっている" moment right after もつ's picture debut. But
the lesson's own two headline words never get a recognition check: a learner
who can produce "ドアを あけては いけません" perfectly by the end of the
lesson has still never once been asked "what does あける mean?" in the
cheap, low-stakes direction. Given the rule card explicitly chose the
pairing over individual picture debuts, this reads as a real gap rather than
an acceptable tradeoff.

**`ja-m14-neo-review-3`:** clean and appropriately paced; correctly surfaces
all four pack verbs in dictionary form for their SRS write (あける/しめる/
もつ/ひく), and even documents in its own IR comment a real tokenizer trap
it worked around (a trailing ？ eating the last token's SRS credit — "ひく？
never credited hiku").
