## Lesson 29 — Wa-row — Intro 2 (ja-m1-wa-2)

1. [grammar_rule] RULE CARD — を — the particle kana
   を is pronounced 'o' and is used only as a grammar particle — it marks the object of an action. It appears between words, never starting one.
