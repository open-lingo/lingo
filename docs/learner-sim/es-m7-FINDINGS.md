# ES m7 — learner-sim findings (combined walk, 2026-08-25)

One Opus fresh-learner combined walk of the Sonnet-dispatched m7 (café
module). Praise: "the café is a real place", the dialogue sims the best
steps, both-correct offers "genuinely accepting both". Verdict headline:
the quiero-un / me-gusta-el rule was stated as an article LAW the module
itself then "broke" for eight lessons. Fix round applied same session.

## Fixed
- **The article rule contradiction (walk's #1)** — L2's card claimed
  "un for now, el for always / me gusta + the el-word", then quiero+el
  appeared everywhere. Card now tells the truth: «me gusta» always
  keeps el/la (even where English drops 'the'); «quiero» takes un for
  one-now AND el/la for a specific thing («quiero el café» — that one).
  Description's "un for now, el for always" cut. Generic glosses
  aligned: «me gusta la manzana» = "I like apples" (both steps).
- **L2 offer-sim trap** — goal said "Say you want the coffee" while
  marking «me gusta el café» wrong → goal is now "Order a coffee —
  right now."
- **Bill called delicious** — L6 payoff sim replied ¡qué rico! to the
  arriving check → turns reordered: Carmen's «¿Todo bien?» (→ ¡qué
  rico!) BEFORE Diego's «¡Hay dinero! La cuenta...» (→ ¡gracias,
  Diego!, goal "He's paying — thank him!").
- **María serving at Carmen's café** (L10) → scene moved to the café
  by the bus depot, where María helps out on Saturdays.
- **te gusta asked 3× but never taught** → L3 (the flip lesson) now
  teaches it as the me-gusta mirror in the card, prints «¿te gusta el
  café?» (speaking voicing) and builds «¿te gusta el pan?».
- **Glued-tile tell** — compiled builds split the TARGET phrase into
  word tiles but left phrase DISTRACTORS glued ("tengo sed"/"me gusta"
  one-piece = always wrong). All three m7 banks now use single-word
  distractors; class-checked across m4–m7 IRs (m7-only).
- **Recall monoculture** (1 of 12 recalls was m7 material) → five
  swapped to module voicings: «me gusta el café» (L5), «tengo sed»
  (L7), «tengo hambre» (L8), «¿quieres un café?» (L9), «quiero un
  café» (L10); the mi-familia and ¿dónde-está? duplicates gone.
- **Bare-noun debut unexplained** («___ pan» after two article-drilled
  lessons) → the first bare-food cloze's why now states the pattern:
  with food, Spanish often skips the article.
- **L1 gifts/oddities** — vocab MCQ got a drinkable distractor (el
  agua); Carmen's opener no longer uses pre-taught «quieres» («¿Café o
  té?»); L4 card now glosses hambre/sed literally ('I have hunger');
  sim's untaught «también» → «¿Y tienes sed?».
- **"a bread"** (L7 map) → «quiero un café y pan», map English reads
  "and bread".
- **cp-cloze-2 non-thought** («quiero un pan pero tengo sed») →
  «me gusta el pan pero quiero sopa».
- **Summit tile-order ambiguity** — both L9/L10 check-builds now also
  accept «la cuenta por favor no gracias» orders (max-acceptance).
- **Ana promised, never spoke** (L9 setting) → Sofía alone at the
  table.
- **la sopa never an ear target** → L7's review ear now targets it.
