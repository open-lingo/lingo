**m15** a relative clause is a plain sentence parked in front of a noun; no の between them · the clause verb stays PLAIN, ます only on the last verb · clause can be past · the clause's own subject takes が, never は · こと nominalises a verb (= の) and also means "thing (abstract)" · とき = "when": verb plain, noun + の, な-adj + な; keep both halves the same tense · まえに ALWAYS takes the dictionary form whatever the tense; noun + の · てから = "after doing", て-form + から. **Never carded: 「ミカが すきな えいが」, a な-adjective heading a relative clause — but required in eight builds.**
