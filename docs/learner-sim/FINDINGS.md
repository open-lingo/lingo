# Learner-simulation findings

Appended as the simulated student walks the course. One line per finding.

Severity:
- **BLOCKER** — a step cannot be answered from what the course has taught. A
  word never introduced, a form never explained, an answer that needs
  knowledge from a later module.
- **CONFUSING** — answerable, but a real person would hesitate or guess. Two
  defensible answers, a rule card that does not cover the case, a jump in
  difficulty with no bridge.
- **NIT** — noticed, not painful.
