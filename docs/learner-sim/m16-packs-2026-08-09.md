## Findings

### Finding 1 — MAJOR: the render-time build-tile pad leaks words the course won't teach for 3–12 more modules (or ever) into the walked lessons' tile banks

**Where:** six pad-added distractor tiles across the three target lessons,
each a word no live lesson has taught by m16:

- `ja-m16-neo-11-s-4` ("スポーツは からだに いい"): **コンビニ** — taught by
  NOTHING in the live course. Its registry row (`courseAtoms.ts:287`) is
  attributed to `ja-m12-kata`, a RETIRED old-course katakana lesson on no
  map tile; the live neo katakana rows are glyph-only
  (`katakanaRows.ts:683`, `ctxFor(rowId, [])`), and コンビニ appears in zero
  IR modules. It is the plan's own pack-10 (m19) word.
- `ja-m16-neo-review-3-s-18` and `-s-20` (the はしる and よわい pack
  beats): **がっこう** ×2 — first taught m19 (`ir/m19.ir.yaml:344`).
- `ja-m16-neo-review-3-s-0`: **こうえん** — introduced by no IR module at
  all (B065 class).
- `ja-m16-neo-review-3-s-18`: **へや** — first taught m27
  (`ir/m27.ir.yaml:476`).
- `ja-m16-neo-review-3-s-1`: **まいにち** — first taught m28
  (`ir/m28.ir.yaml:452`).
- `ja-m16-neo-review-3-s-6`: **ひるごはん** — introduced by no IR module.

**Root cause (engine, not pack authoring):** the compiled steps are clean —
verified by diffing raw `compileModule(m16Ir)` output against
`getMockLessonContent` output: every word distractor in all three lessons
is added at render time by `padBuildTileFloor`
(`mockLessons.ts:748`), whose fill pool is `getAtomsUpToModule`
(`buildTileFloor.ts:142`) — a filter on registry `fromModule`
(`lessonAtomIndex.ts:254-257`). The pad's own header claims "PRIOR-ONLY …
the same 'never introduce a not-yet-taught word' invariant"
(`buildTileFloor.ts:34-36`), but `fromModule` tags are stale by
construction — the exact trap `moduleCompiler.ts:683-687` documents and
the IR `priorVocab` plumbing was built to kill on the compiler side. The
leak resurfaces the same bug class through the render pass, and
`buildTileFloor.test.ts` never checks fill against the live taught-set.

**Fairness note:** these are reject-only tiles — never correct, never
graded, and by m16 the learner reads both scripts, so コンビニ is at least
decodable. But a learner pausing on がっこう ("wait, have I learned
'school'? ") is being shown a word the course has deliberately withheld
until m19, and the pad picks are seeded-deterministic, so every learner
sees exactly these. Course-wide shared infrastructure (every module ≥m6 is
exposed), evidenced here because this walk diffed pre-pad vs post-pad
banks; fix belongs in the pad (hand it the IR priorVocab or the unlock
index, not `fromModule`).

### Finding 2 — NIT: め (eye) rides pack 6 as a de-facto new word, untracked by every guard, because priorVocab mistakes the m1 GLYPH for the word

**Where:** `ja-m16-neo-11-s-5` (listening_build, "けいたいは めに わるい",
credits `me`) and the rule card's example/antiPattern pair. め is in
neither `introduces` (`ir/m16.ir.yaml:800`) nor `newAtoms`; its only
teaching is the rule card's prose gloss ("めに わるい, bad for the eyes")
and example translation. No debut, no recognition beat, no ratchet
candidacy (the ratchet walks `introduces` only —
`recognitionExposure.test.ts:83`), one graded touch, production.

**Root cause:** `compile-ir.mjs:109`'s quoted-hit scan
(`corpus.includes('"め"')`) has no length floor, and `"め"` appears in
earlier hand-authored lessons as a SPELLING GLYPH in character-granularity
arrays (`m3-neo.ts:631` `["め", "が", "ね"]` for めがね; `m5-neo-a.ts:578`
same; `m3-neo.ts:1320` はじめまして) — so `priorVocab` lists め as met
vocabulary though no live lesson ever taught the word "eye". Every
single-kana word (え, き, て…) can leak through the same hole.

**Softeners, which is why this is a NIT and not a MAJOR:** the choice is
knowing and documented — `courseAtoms.ts:788` re-homed `me` to m16 with an
explicit note ("First exercised by ja-m16-neo-11's けいたいは めに わるい …
m22 gives it the full body-lesson debut"), the rule card genuinely glosses
it, the listening_build tile is audible ("me") and scaffolded, and the D2
gate keeps content-lesson grades off it (fromModule = own module). The
generalizable hazard is the guard blindness, not this learner's experience.

### Finding 5 — NIT: pack 5's rule-card example romaji renders クラス un-romanized ("クラスha kujikara juujimadeda")

## What the learner actually experiences

**`ja-m16-neo-10`:** the strongest-authored pack lesson the wave has
produced. The rule card spends the module's own machinery on the new words
(クラス takes から…まで, いみ takes ので, かかなかった closes L8's paradigm)
so nothing feels bolted on; the four picture words debut image-first
directly before their first build; and the recognition clozes land right
after the corresponding builds, so the cheap direction is always offered.
A learner who missed よむ vs かく on the cloze gets an explanation that
names all three verbs. The one rough edge a learner could meet is
infrastructural (finding 1 puts no untaught tile in THIS lesson — its pad
picks all resolve to known words).

**`ja-m16-neo-11`:** the からだに/めに わるい frame is genuinely well
chosen — it recycles に in a new sense the antiPattern makes explicit, and
the たいせつな…ので beat spends the module's headline trap on a pack word.
すわない-before-すう ordering is honest (the card teaches the negative as
the refusal formula; the cloze then asks for the plain habit against it).
め is the soft spot: it is glossed in prose, then spelled from audio, and
never touched again this module — workable, but thinner than anything else
here (finding 2). The コンビニ tile (finding 1) is a one-tile blemish.

**`ja-m16-neo-review-3`:** a fair consolidation that interleaves the pack
block with the module's whole inventory rather than ghettoizing it, and
its SRS bookkeeping is exactly what the pack architecture promises — all
15 words' dictionary forms credited, statements only. The pad's untaught
tiles cluster here (four of six instances) because R3's authored banks are
lean and the floor pass tops every build up to 3 distractors.
