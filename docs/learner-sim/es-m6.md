# ES module 6 — the learner's view

You are seeing exactly what a learner sees, in order. Answers are
stripped; options and tiles are shuffled. Audio is written as the
text a learner HEARS (they cannot read it unless a step shows it).
On MEANING-prompted word-choice (MCQ) steps, tapping any option
plays its audio before you commit. On hear-it steps (the audio IS
the question) the options stay SILENT until you answer — your ear
and the spelling are all you have.
The course language is Spanish.

## Big or small — say it

1. [info] CARD — Pink and blue, at work
   Since m3, la-words glow pink and el-words glow blue. Now you'll meet describing words: some flip an ending to match the word they describe, others never change at all. Today: «es» + a describing word — «es» says what something IS; «está» from m4 keeps its job of saying WHERE it is.

2. [word_map] MAP THE SENTENCE (audio plays: "la casa es grande")
   English, highlighted one at a time: the · house · is · big
   Spanish chips: la | es | casa | grande
   (each English word in turn: tap the Spanish word it maps to; solved chips lock in with their translation)
   As chips solve they take a color + tiny letter: la pink·f, casa pink·f — untinted words stay neutral

3. [speaking] Say aloud: «es grande»  (it's big)
