# KO frequency + fluency-level foundation — research (2026-08-26)

**Goal:** give the Korean course the same corpus-frequency + fluency-level
foundation JA has (docs/data/ja-top500-cejc.json, ja-core6k-order.json,
ja-neo-vocab.json + the frequency-atom drip), ahead of the KO quality

## 1. What KO already has (repo audit)

### 1.1 The registry: `src/features/languages/ko/frequencyAtoms.ts`

- **2,998 atoms**, generated by `scripts/ingest-ko-frequency.mjs` (332 lines,
  committed `0df0da31`). Do-not-hand-edit; regeneration is the command of
  record.
- **Provenance (sound, and better than JA's runtime registry):**
  - Spine: **국립국어원 한국어 학습용 어휘 목록 (2003)** — 5,965 graded
    learner words (surface / POS / Hanja / grade A-B-C). KOGL Type 1.
  - Ranking: **국립국어원 현대 국어 사용 빈도 조사 (2002)**
    (`단어_빈도색인.txt`, 58,437 words with raw counts), joined by surface;
    fallback to the learner list's own 순위. KOGL Type 1 (+ AI badge).
  - `reading` = generated RR (`romanizeKorean`), `meaningEn` = curated
    glosses (`scripts/data/ko-glosses.json`, ~2,998 of 5,965 glossed — words
    without an accurate gloss are omitted, not guessed).
  - `unlockModule` baked from the shared `frequencyRankToModule` with
    `lastModule: KO_FREQ_LAST_MODULE = 27`.
- Conjugation-linked: 82 atoms (exact `KO_LEMMAS` surface matches,
  predicates only).
- **Contrast with JA:** JA's runtime `ja/frequencyAtoms.ts` has *no* corpus
  ranks — it uses registry-order as a proxy over `fromModule: "future"`
  course atoms. JA's corpus data lives in `docs/data/*.json` and feeds
  *authoring* (spine planning, gap audits, `scripts/authoring-context.mjs`).
  KO has the opposite shape: corpus-ranked runtime registry, **zero
  docs/data authoring layer**. `docs/data/` contains only the three `ja-*`
  files — nothing for ko.

### 1.2 Gating: how `frequencyRankToModule` treats ko

`src/features/languages/frequencyTypes.ts`: first module 3, 20 words/module,
`FREQ_LAST_MODULE_DEFAULT = 27` (which *is* KO's ceiling; JA passes 30).
Measured unlockModule distribution in the KO registry: exactly 20 atoms per
module m3–m26 (480 dripped), and **2,518 atoms (84%) piled at m27** — the
documented overflow-at-last-module design. Fine for an "optional vocab"
drip; useless as a curriculum budget. Any module-count change in the KO
re-author requires re-running the ingest (see 1.4).

### 1.3 Defects found in the current frequencyRank (provenance sound, join flawed)

1. **Homograph smearing.** The ingest strips the corpus's homograph digits
   (`가구03` → `가구`) and **sums counts per bare surface**; every
   learner-list sense of that surface then inherits the summed count and
   gets adjacent ranks. Result: 이 "tooth" (rank 11) and 이 "louse" (rank
   12) sit in the top 20 because the surface 이 (demonstrative/number) is
   frequent; likewise 말 "horse" #39, 한 "grudge" #36, 씨 "seed" #49,
   수 "number" #10. Measured: the top-500 rank slots cover only **389
   distinct surfaces** — ~22% of top-500 slots are extra homograph senses
   riding a frequent surface. These rare senses unlock absurdly early in the
   drip. Fix: join on the digit-suffixed key (the 2002 file carries the same
   digit convention plus 풀이/품사 columns) or on (surface, POS) — an ingest
   change, needs the raw files back (1.4).
2. **Written register.** The 2002 survey is overwhelmingly written-corpus.
   It is KO's Core6k analogue, **not** its CEJC analogue: 그러나 at rank 48
   is the tell (spoken Korean says 근데/그런데). The JA audit showed exactly
   how far written and spoken ranks diverge (うん = spoken #1, Core6k
   #2505). KO currently has no spoken signal at all.
3. **Grade A/B/C is parsed and thrown away.** `loadLearnerSpine()` reads the
   등급 column; the emitter never outputs it. The fluency-level foundation
   passes through the ingest and is discarded. Re-emitting is a few lines —
   but see 1.4.
4. **`--candidates` output not in repo.** `ko-frequency-candidates.json`
   (which would carry grade + corpusFreq per word) was never committed.

### 1.4 Reproducibility gap (blocking, but cheap)

`.gitignore:191` ignores `scripts/data/ko-frequency-raw/` "reproducible via
SOURCES.txt" — but **SOURCES.txt lives inside the ignored dir**, and the dir
does not exist on this machine. So today the ingest **cannot be re-run**:
the raw NIKL files must be re-downloaded (URLs preserved in
`docs/ko-6k-vocab-sourcing-2026-07-24.md` §A/§B — korean.go.kr etc_seq=71
and etc_seq=61). No licensing decision needed (already cleared, KOGL
Type 1), just a re-download. Recommend also moving SOURCES.txt out of the
ignored dir (e.g. `scripts/data/ko-frequency-raw.SOURCES.md`, committed).

### 1.5 Course side: `ko/courseAtoms.ts` + curriculum

- 311 `kind: "vocab"` atoms (362 distinct surfaces incl. jamo/syllable/
  phrase/particle kinds); ids `ko:<surface>` with `-NN` homograph suffixes —
  **the same id convention as the frequency registry** (0 id collisions
  measured, and shared SRS key space by design).
- Curriculum: m1/m2 Hangul + m3–m27 content (27 module files); the audit
  found m3 needs work; quality uplevel in progress.

## 2. Gap-analysis feasibility (taught vs corpus top-N)

**Feasible today, repo-internal, zero downloads** — the corpus ranking is
already baked into the registry. Quick measurement (surface-level match of
courseAtoms surfaces against registry ranks):

| Corpus slice | distinct surfaces | taught (surface match) | coverage |
|---|---|---|---|
| top-100 | 72 | 23 | 32% |
| top-300 | 231 | 56 | 24% |
| top-500 | 389 | 78 | 20% |
| top-1000 | 821 | 123 | 15% |
| all 2,998 | 2,703 | 207 | 8% |

JA at its 2026-07-19 audit: 278/442 top-500 content words taught (63%).
**KO's written-top-500 coverage is ~20% — a much bigger hole than JA had**,
consistent with 25 content modules vs JA's 30 and the thinner per-module
vocab. Caveats: surface matching misses phrase-taught words and conjugated
forms (JA hand-checked the same artifacts); homograph smearing (1.3.1)
inflates the denominator by ~20%, so true sense-level coverage of the top
~380 distinct surfaces is nearer 78/389 ≈ 20–26%. Good enough to rank
holes; the real audit should replicate the JA method (matcher + hand-check,
strong-findings-only writeup).

What's *missing* for the full JA-style analysis: a **spoken** top-N (the
2002 list is the wrong register for a conversational course) and the
level grades (to separate "missing A-grade word" = urgent from "missing
C-grade word" = fine).

## 4. Proposal

### 4.1 New files under `docs/data/` (authoring layer, mirrors JA)

1. **`docs/data/ko-graded-vocab.json`** — the `ja-neo-vocab.json` analogue
   and the workhorse. One entry per learner-list word (homograph-keyed):

   ```json
   {
     "surface": "보다", "homographKey": "보다01", "reading": "boda",
     "pos": "verb", "hanja": null, "meaningEn": "to see / to look",
     "gradeAbc": "A", "intlLevel": 1,
     "freqRank2002": 6, "corpusCount": 12345,
     "spokenRank": null,
     "currentModule": "m5", "tags": ["in-course", "grade-A"],
     "priority": 6
   }
   ```

   Sources: learner list (surface/POS/Hanja/grade) + 2002 counts (fixed,
   homograph-aware join) + 국제통용 level (join on lemma+POS; expect ~90%+
   overlap on the A/B tiers, keep both fields — they disagree usefully) +
   `currentModule` attributed from courseAtoms/curriculum the way
   ja-neo-vocab does. `spokenRank` stays null until the Modu decision.
   ~5,965 rows (not just the 2,998 glossed ones — ungapped input for gap
   detection; `meaningEn` null where unglossed).

2. **`docs/data/ko-top500-spoken.json`** — the `ja-top500-cejc.json`
   analogue (`{rank, lemma, reading, pos, gloss}`), **blocked on the Modu
   agreement**. Pipeline once unblocked: download 일상 대화 말뭉치 →
   morphologically analyze (kiwipiepy — LGPL-3.0, fine as a *local tooling*
   dependency since nothing ships; or mecab-ko + mecab-ko-dic) → lemma
   frequency over content morphemes → top-500 with hand-checked glosses.
   The corpus never enters the repo; only the derived list does (pending
   약정서 reading).

3. **No `ko-core6k-order.json` needed** — the registry itself already *is*
   the frequency-ordered core list; ko-graded-vocab.json carries the ranks.

### 4.2 Fluency levels in the registry

Add optional fields to the KO emit (NOT a schema fork — `FrequencyAtom` can
grow optional `grade?: string` or keep it KO-side): re-emit `grade`
("A"|"B"|"C") and optionally `intlLevel` (1–6) from the ingest, since the
data is already parsed there. Runtime uses: level filters in the vocab
viewer/deck ("show me A-grade words I haven't met"), and the SRS intake
prioritizer. Authoring-time leveling stays in ko-graded-vocab.json — same
division as JA (docs/data for planning, registry for runtime). Requires the
raw re-download (1.4) before any re-emit; fold the homograph-join fix
(1.3.1) into the same re-ingest so ranks and grades land together.

### 4.3 What the KO re-author consumes these for

- **Module word budgets:** spine-planning à la `docs/spine-n4.md` D14 —
  each re-authored module draws its vocab from the graded pool
  (grade-A ∩ high-frequency first), instead of ad-hoc word choice.
- **Gap detection:** `scripts/authoring-context.mjs` pattern — rankByLemma
  map from ko-graded-vocab.json (later ko-top500-spoken.json), flag
  "frequent but untaught/starved" words per module. The §2 table is the
  v0 of this.
- **Level-appropriate sentence vocab:** the comprehensibility gate stays
  atom-based, but drafting prompts get a hard floor: no above-level
  (grade B/C or intlLevel > tier) content word in a beginner-tier sentence
  unless it's the taught target — the structural-floor lesson from
  [[vocab-gate-blind-to-fragments]] applied to KO.

### 4.4 KO-specific modeling decisions (settle before re-ingest)

- **Particle-attached surfaces:** non-issue for the NIKL lists (lemmatized;
  조사/어미 shipped as separate files — consider ingesting those for a
  particle/ending ordering signal). Real issue only for any
  subtitle/Modu-derived list → must lemmatize (kiwi/mecab-ko), strip josa,
  and drop non-Hangul noise before counting.
- **하다-verbs:** the learner list carries 공부 and 공부하다 as separate
  entries with separate counts. Keep them separate atoms (matches current
  registry + conjugation engine, which links 하다-class lemmas), but the
  gap analysis should treat `N하다` as covering `N` for coverage purposes
  (a taught 공부하다 means 공부 isn't a "hole").
- **Honorific/humble variants (계시다/있다, 드리다/주다, 말씀/말, 진지/밥):**
  separate lemmas in the source lists → keep as separate atoms; use the
  level grades to order them (plain form at its own rank, honorific
  typically B-grade). Do not collapse to one lemma — Korean treats them as
  distinct words and the SRS should too.
- **Homographs:** move the whole pipeline to the digit-suffixed homograph
  key end-to-end (glosses file already uses it; the atoms' `-NN` id suffix
  already preserves it) — that is also the smearing fix.
