Two consequences for the order: **querer** moves to the first lesson of m2 (it unlocks five of
the top survival lines), and **question words** come before the full -ar paradigm (three survival
questions need only the word plus é/custa/fica). Chunks marked "chunk" are taught unanalysed on a
`phrase` step in the lesson named, and re-taught as grammar later — the JA kana-era precedent.

## 3. Retention hooks (content-side; product features flagged separately)

1. **One payoff line per lesson.** Every lesson's win sentence is a §2 phrase the learner can use
   that day, and the lesson description says which ("After this lesson you can order a coffee").
   Machine rule: `win:` must appear in the §2 table or the spec fails.
2. **A running story with a cliffhanger.** Sam arrives in São Paulo (m1), moves into Bia's
   building (m2), plans the trip to the Rio coast with Pedro (m3), takes it (m4). Every teaching
   lesson's sim ends on a next-step line from the cast («Amanhã a gente vai à praia?») that the
   next lesson's map opens with. Cast stays fixed: Sam (learner), Bia, Pedro, Rafael, plus one new
   speaker per module (m2 a vendor, m3 a driver, m4 a guide).
3. **Recall before new.** Every lesson's map re-uses the previous lesson's win line as its first
   sentence, and every lesson carries two `recall:` words from L−2 and L−3, which is the spacing
   the existing FSRS deck already reinforces. Checkpoints (L6) teach zero words and grade the
   module's contrast pairs only.
4. **Cognate and false-friend beats.** One free-win cognate per lesson where the bank has one
   (rank ≤ 2,000), one false-friend contrast per module (pasta, puxar, esquisito, embaraçada).
5. Product-side (Spencer's call, not content): a personal phrasebook that collects win lines with
   their audio for replay, and a "you can now say N phrases" counter on the course card. ~1 day.

## 4. Lesson spine, m1–m4 (words = bank lemma, rank in parentheses; filled from PTBANK)

Per lesson: grammar point · new words (≤8; nouns need an emoji) · recall · contrast pair (ok / wrong)
· scene and payoff line. m1 rows are the shipped lessons, listed for the recall chain only.

rows (casar 95, aguar 367, horar 198, coisar 85) — the real nouns rank there.

## 5. Machine form

`scripts/author/pt/spine/pt-spine.yaml` — one entry per lesson with the same fields as §4, consumed
by `from-spec.mjs` via a `spine:` reference so a lesson spec inherits `words:`, `recall:`,
`contrast:` and `win:` and the writing lane supplies only `sentences:` and `dialogue:`.
