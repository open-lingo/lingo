## §2 Engineering — the central judgment call: ruleset yes, production step no

### The ruleset question, resolved

`TRANSFORM_RULESETS`/`RULESET_ALTERNATES` (`src/features/languages/ja/conjugation/
transformRulesets.ts`) currently carry exactly **7 keys**: `nai, masu, te, volitional, ba, passive,
causative`. **No `imperative`/`prohibitive` entry exists.** `ChainForm`
(`conjugationEngine.ts:15–35`, 20 members, confirmed by direct read — no `zu`/`zuni` member exists at
all) already has both `imperative` and `prohibitive` as live derivation targets, each carrying a
`// UNTAUGHT` comment pointing at `provider.ts`'s `FREE_DRILL_VERB_FORM_MODULE`. The derivation
switch-case (`conjugateVerb`, lines 189/204/218/241/252/260–262) already fully implements both forms
— **the engine is ready; only the teaching-table half is missing.**

`docs/authoring-invariants-pinned.md` inv 49 ("Formation points") states literally: "A formation
point ships with a rule table AND a transform card, or it does not ship" — and explicitly names
命令形/禁止形 as an N4-tier formation point this applies to.

**But the course's own precedent directly contradicts inv 49's literal text for a genuinely
analogous case.** I read `ir/m25.ir.yaml` directly: だろう — also a recognition-only,
register-hazardous form (plain conjecture, "mostly heard, not said," per its own registered gloss at
line 375) — shipped with **zero** `TRANSFORM_RULESETS` entry (confirmed: grep for `darou` in
`transformRulesets.ts` returns nothing) and **zero** `conjugation_transform` steps. It's taught
entirely via a plain `{ kind: rule, grammarPointId: darou }` card (line 303) and sentence beats
(`exercises: [darou, ...]`, lines 612–613), with だろう itself registered as an ordinary `kind: vocab`
atom. D8's own citation list (`docs/spine-n4.md` §5) names だろう (m25) as exactly this precedent,
alongside ぼく/おれ and ら抜き.

**This is a genuine unresolved tension, and I am stating my judgment call rather than picking
silently:**

- **Recommendation: add `TRANSFORM_RULESETS.imperative` and `.prohibitive`, but never author a
  `conjugation_transform` step for either form.** Reasoning: inv 49 is a *pinned* invariant that
  explicitly names these two forms by name — a pinned doc naming something specifically is a stronger
  signal than an unpinned precedent (だろう) that predates the invariant's own writing and may simply
  not have been revisited against it. Adding the ruleset costs nothing risky: it only enables
  `TransformRuleTable` to be embedded inside a `grammarRule` step via its `conjugationForm` field
  (`GrammarRuleStepView.tsx` lines 395, 451) — a **display-only** path with **no** connection to the
  `conjugation_transform` step type's stage-ramp mechanism. The rule table shows the full
  のめ／たべろ／しろ／こい and のむな／たべるな／するな／くるな conjugation grids for recognition
  study, satisfying inv 49's literal "ships with a rule table" clause, while `conjugation_transform`
  — the ONLY step type that can reach typed production — is simply never invoked for these two keys.
- **Why not the だろう precedent instead (skip the ruleset entirely):** the counter-argument is real —
  だろう shipped clean with none of this machinery, suggesting the course doesn't actually enforce
  inv 49 for recognition-only formation points in practice. But だろう has no engine-native
  conjugation TABLE to show in the first place (it's a single invariant suffix, not a verb-class ×
  form grid) — 命令形/禁止形 genuinely ARE verb-class-conditioned paradigms (のむ→のめ, たべる→たべろ,
  する→しろ, くる→こい — four distinct patterns), which is exactly the shape `TransformRuleTable` is
  built to display and exactly the shape inv 49 is written to cover. The two precedents aren't
  actually parallel once the "does this form even have a table" question is asked. **Ship the
  ruleset.**

### The mechanism risk this decision must respect

`transformCells.ts`'s `getTransformStage()` promotes any (form × verb-class) cell to Stage 3 (typed
production) after ≥2 consolidated cross-day recognition reps — **entirely independent of
`FREE_DRILL_VERB_FORM_MODULE`**, which only gates the visibility of the standalone Conjugation
Trainer page's toggle, not in-lesson step authoring. `FREE_DRILL_VERB_FORM_MODULE`'s existing
`Infinity` setting for `imperative`/`prohibitive` protects the free-drill surface correctly, but it
does **nothing** to stop a `conjugation_transform` step authored inside m47's own IR from reaching
typed production the moment a learner racks up 2 reps. **The only real gate is "never author the step
type" — there is no config flag to lean on.** This is the single most load-bearing engineering fact
in this brief: get it wrong and D8's production ban is silently violated by the IR itself, not by any
missing config.

### なさい, ないで, ずに — three different engine-support levels, budget accordingly

- **〜なさい** rides the **existing masu-stem** derivation site (established since m4/m5, the same
  site m46's compound-verb trio and m4's original ます-form both use). No new `ChainForm` member, no
  new ruleset. Cheapest item in the module engineering-wise.
- **〜ないで** (linking/"without doing" sense) rides the **existing `nai` ChainForm/ruleset** — no new
  ruleset work. **But it is NOT a fresh grammar point in isolation**: m14 already shipped a grammar
  point named `naide-kudasai` (`ir/m14.ir.yaml` line 262) teaching ないでください as a FUSED,
  non-decomposed polite-register fixed expression ("the voice of signs, teachers and rules," per its
  own rule text) — explicitly "No `kind: register` beat, no politeness meter." m47's ないで item is a
  **different sense** of the same surface string (the adverbial "without doing X, [do Y]" linking
  use, e.g. ぼうしを かぶらないで でかけた — "went out without wearing a hat" — not a request). The
  m47 grammar point must be named and worded to avoid a learner conflating it with m14's
  ないでください — propose `naide-without` as the id (deliberately NOT `naide-request`, to keep it
  visually distinct from `naide-kudasai` in any diff/grep), and its rule card should explicitly
  cross-reference `naide-kudasai` by id ("you've seen ないで before, asking someone not to do
  something — here the same ない-form + で links two actions instead").
- **〜ずに** has **zero ChainForm/engine support** — confirmed no `zu`/`zuni` member exists anywhere
  in the 20-member `ChainForm` type. It must be fully hand-authored as `kind: verb-form` newAtoms
  with no derivation ground-truth to check against (unlike なさい/ないで, which ride tables the engine
  already validates). せずに is the spine's own named irregular (する→せずに, not しずに) and needs
  explicit hand-registration and explicit callout in the rule card as the one exception to the regular
  V-ないform-minus-ない+ずに pattern. **Budget this as the highest-authoring-risk single item in the
  module** — every ずに surface actually drilled needs individual verification against no engine
  safety net.

## §6 Explicitly rejected approaches

1. **Skipping the `TRANSFORM_RULESETS.imperative`/`.prohibitive` entries entirely, citing だろう
   (m25) as course precedent for recognition-only formation points.** Rejected as the default,
   though it's a real counter-argument (§2): だろう has no engine-native conjugation TABLE to show in
   the first place (a single invariant suffix, not a verb-class-conditioned paradigm), so it isn't
   actually parallel to 命令形/禁止形, which ARE four-pattern verb-class paradigms — exactly the shape
   `TransformRuleTable` exists to display and exactly what inv 49 (which explicitly names these two
   forms) is written to require. Ship the ruleset; keep it display-only via `grammarRule`'s
   `conjugationForm` field, never via `conjugation_transform`.
2. **Authoring a `conjugation_transform` step for imperative/prohibitive and relying on
   `FREE_DRILL_VERB_FORM_MODULE`'s `Infinity` setting to keep production banned.** Rejected — that
   config only gates the standalone Conjugation Trainer page's toggle visibility. It has no
   connection to `transformCells.ts`'s `getTransformStage()`, which promotes any drilled cell to
   typed production (Stage 3) after 2 consolidated cross-day recognition reps regardless of any
   module-level or page-level config. Authoring this step type for these two forms would silently
   violate D8's production ban from inside the IR itself. The only real gate is never authoring the
   step type.
3. **Tile-decomposing きをつける into き/を/つける for a standard particle-cloze/tile-bank
   treatment.** Rejected: き collides with the registered 木 ("tree," m18) atom and つける collides
   with the registered future-tagged つける ("turn on") atom, both on bare-kana-key homograph grounds
   (§1c). Register as a single fused expression atom instead, mirroring the treatment already given
   to other multi-kana fixed expressions (m46's だんだん/いっしゅん-class single-string atoms).
4. **Registering 〜ないで as a brand-new grammar point unrelated to m14's `naide-kudasai`.**
   Rejected: it is the SAME surface string in a DIFFERENT sense (linking "without doing X" vs. the
   request "please don't"), and a learner who has already internalized ないでください as a fixed
   polite expression needs the new sense explicitly bridged, not silently re-taught as if novel. The
   `naide-without` id and its cross-referencing rule-card text are chosen specifically to avoid this
   confusion (§2).
5. **Teaching なさい, 命令形, and 禁止形 back-to-back in lessons 3–4–5 for "clean register-family
   parallelism."** Rejected: violates interleave-dont-block-teach doctrine, and specifically risky
   here because these three forms are exactly the register-confusable set the pedagogy requirement
   warns about — stacking them invites a learner to blur which register goes with which form. L2 and
   L5 are placed as genuine unrelated breaks (きをつける vocab, あぶない cash-in) between the
   register-ladder items precisely to prevent this.
6. **Assembling the six-register speech-act ladder early (e.g. as its own teaching lesson once all
   six forms have individually debuted) rather than waiting for R3.** Rejected: the spine explicitly
   states the ladder is "assembled only in the final review" — treating it as a standalone teaching
   point would require re-introducing content already taught, inflating the module's own graded
   surface for no pedagogical gain, and preempting R3's actual job.
7. **Hand-registering every reachable conjugated form of すわる/たつ/ならぶ/いそぐ/やめる "just in
   case," now that 〜なさい needs their masu-stems.** Rejected: masu-stems are already a first-class
   auto-derived form since m4/m5 (unlike the compound-verb/causative-form risk m45/m46 carried) — no
   extra hand-registration is needed for the masu-stem site itself. Only the specific なさい-form
   surfaces actually drilled in an authored beat need hand-registration as `verb-form` newAtoms,
   mirroring m45/m46's own budget discipline.
8. **Falling back to N3 spine reasoning.** Rejected: the N4 spine is not exhausted — m47 is n4-18 of
   51 mapped modules, directly following m46 (n4-17) with no gap in `docs/spine-n4.md`.
