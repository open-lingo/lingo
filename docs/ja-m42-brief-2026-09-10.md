## 0. What m42 teaches (from spine-n4.md, corrected below)

- **Three hearsay/report mechanisms, deliberately NOT taught as one grammar
  point:**
  - **〜そうだ (hearsay)**: plain-form clause + そうだ, reporting what one
    heard — あめが ふるそうです ("I hear it's going to rain"). Distinct from
    m36's **appearance そう** (stem + そう, ふりそう "looks like it'll
    rain") by attachment site alone: appearance そう attaches to the verb
    stem/adjective stem (drops い, drops だ); hearsay そうだ attaches to the
    FULL plain/dictionary form. This is the tell the spine flags six
    modules in advance (m36's own notes: "the only tell that will later
    separate it from hearsay そうだ, six modules out").
  - **〜って (casual quotative)**: reported speech in casual register,
    田中さんが来るって ("Tanaka said he's coming") — functionally ≈ と言って
    いた, compressed. `courseAtoms.ts` already carries a defensive stub for
    this (`id: "p-tte"`, `fromModule: "m18"`) that has never been live
    content — see §1.
  - **〜らしい (hearsay/inference from indirect evidence)**: plain-form +
    らしい, weaker/more distanced than そうだ — あの店、おいしいらしいよ
    ("apparently that shop is good [I heard/it seems]"). New word, new
    grammar point, no prior stub.
- **と言っていた (reported speech, dictionary-register precursor)**: the
  spine's `salvage` note explicitly says "m18's とおもう/という unit... are
  the assets" — m42 is meant to extend m18's `to-quotation` grammar point
  (という, dictionary-form quoting) into past/reported territory, not
  invent quoting from scratch.
- **NOT here: the そう-vs-そう minimal pair.** The spine is explicit
  (`n4-07`'s own notes, line ~394): appearance そう (m36) and hearsay そうだ
  (m42) are placed six modules apart ON PURPOSE and meet exactly once, as a
  discrimination drill, at the **m51 capstone** — not at m42. Do not teach
  or drill the contrast here; m42 teaches hearsay そうだ on its own terms.
- **Domain vocabulary**: news/weather/rumor domain — spine `vocab.must`:
  じしん, たいふう, じこ, ばんぐみ, うわさ, きじ; `vocab.prefer`: ニュース,
  しんぶん, ほうそう, きしゃ, はっぴょう, つたえる, しらせる, など;
  `vocab.sidequest`: ひなん, けいほう. `vocab.count: 34` (spine ceiling —

## 2. Engineering: does the compiler/engine already support 〜そうだ/って/らしい?

**Verified 2026-09-10 — NO new engine work is required. All three
constructions are compositional attachments to an already-known plain
form, not new conjugations of the base word, and follow the m30
(ておく)/m38 (てしまう)/m41 (てある) precedent, NOT the m40 (passive)
precedent.**

- `conjugationEngine.ts`'s `ChainForm` type (the exhaustive list of
  morphological transforms the engine derives) has no `sou`/`hearsay`/
  `tte`/`rashii` member — confirmed by direct read (`masu | masu-neg |
  masu-past | masu-past-neg | te | ta | nai | tai | nai-past | tai-neg |
  tai-past | tai-neg-past | volitional | ba | potential | tara |
  imperative | prohibitive | causative | passive`). Nothing needs adding:
  そうだ/って/らしい all attach to the plain/dictionary form (already a
  live `ChainForm` surface since early N5), not a new inflection of the
  base verb/adjective.
- `TRANSFORM_RULESETS` (`conjugation/transformRulesets.ts`) currently has
  exactly 6 entries — `nai, masu, te, volitional, ba, passive` (confirmed
  by direct grep, 2026-09-10 — `passive` is m40's landed 6th entry,
  matching m40's brief). **None of そうだ/って/らしい belong on this list**:
  `docs/authoring-invariants-pinned.md` inv 49's own closed definitional
  list is て, た, ない, ます, potential, passive られる, causative させる,
  volitional よう/おう, ば, 命令形/禁止形 — hearsay/quotative/evidential
  suffixes are not in it, matching m30/m38/m41's compositional precedent
  rather than m40's formation-point precedent.
- **No engine table entries are needed for そうだ/って/らしい themselves**
  (they are invariant suffixes, not conjugating words) — unlike m41's ある,
  which needed hand-authored `verb-form` derivatives because it's itself a
  conjugating helper verb. そうだ/って/らしい attach and (mostly) don't
  conjugate further at N4 (そうです/そうだった are the only forms in scope,
  both hand-authorable as `kind: rule`-adjacent `verb-form`/`adj-form`
  atoms exactly as m36 already does for appearance そう, per §1).
- **Net determination: m42 needs NO changes to `ChainForm`, NO new
  `TRANSFORM_RULESETS` entry.** If a future gate disagrees, the fix is a
  one-line ruleset addition — cheap, not worth blocking dispatch over.
