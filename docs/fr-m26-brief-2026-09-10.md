## 2. Module thesis (design authority for this dispatch)

**m26 is a lexicon-and-seeding module: a tour of the house, then what's in
the kitchen.** The learner already knows how to say what's somewhere
(`il y a`, m4) and what they like (`j'aime`, m3/m11) — this module adds
four rooms and four kitchen staples, using ONLY already-taught
constructions, and deliberately gives the four staples their natural mass
sense so a future module can teach `du`/`de la` against real vocabulary
instead of starting from zero.

The teaching claim to state explicitly in the module header (paste-ready):
"You know how to say what's somewhere («il y a...») and what you like
(«j'aime...»). Let's walk through the house — and open the kitchen."

**Two beats, taught in this order:**
1. **Four rooms (L1–L3):** `chambre`, `salon`, `cuisine`, `jardin` —
   debuted against `il y a un/une [room]` (m4 recall), each countable,
   each taking the ordinary indefinite article, zero new grammar.
2. **Four kitchen staples, mass sense (L4–L7):** `lait`, `eau`, `pain`,
   `beurre` — debuted against `j'aime le/la/l' [staple]` (m3/m4 recall,
   generic reference) and recombined with `il n'y a pas de [staple]` (m4
   recall, negated existence, bare `de`). **Never** `il y a du/de la
   [staple]` anywhere in this module (§2.1) — that is the partitive
   construction itself, still blocked, and this module's whole point is
   to seed the noun inventory, not to teach the grammar early.

### 2.1 Design decision: mass nouns register, partitive grammar does not
ship in m26

A genuine judgment call, recorded with reasoning per the escalation-filter
norm. It would be tempting to let m26 "finish the job" and debut `du`/`de
la` itself, since the vocabulary is right there. **Decision: no.** Three
reasons: (1) `fr-authoring-playbook.md` §6's own stated doctrine treats
the partitive as its own gated beat ("Judgment goes in the inventory")
— conflating vocabulary-seeding with grammar-debut in one module violates
"coverage, not volume" the same way a single module trying to do two
unrelated new-machinery lifts at once would; (2) the `du`/`de la`/`de l'`
gender-and-elision matrix (masculine `du`, feminine `de la`, vowel-onset
`de l'`) is itself a small but real new grammar surface that deserves its
own reasoning-moment lesson, not a tacked-on L8; (3) it keeps m26 a true
lexicon module — zero new grammar machinery, matching the task's own
"lexicon break" framing and the arc-alternation argument in §1.1. **m27
is recommended as the partitive-debut module**, opening against this
module's 4-noun base — named here, not silently assumed.

**Explicitly OUT OF SCOPE for m26** (state these, don't silently omit —
each is a real future candidate, not a rejected idea):
- **Partitive grammar (`du`/`de la`/`de l'`)** — the vocabulary is seeded
  (§1.4/§2.1) but the construction itself is NOT taught here. Recommended
  for m27.
- **Être-aux passé composé extension (arriver/partir/rester, §1.1)** —
  available, correctly priced at 3–6 atoms (correcting m25's 2-atom
  estimate), handed to m27 or m28 on rhythm grounds, not cost grounds.
- **`vous` forms (§1.3)** — still infrastructure-blocked; the concrete
  unblock (a 4th Conjugation Trainer grid-person axis) is now stated
  plainly, not a per-module decision.
- **`on` recombination (§1.2)** — not a real candidate; already
  exhaustively solved since m13, most recently m24.
- **Daily-routine reflexives (se lever/se coucher)** — re-checked: still
  the ONLY hit is m2's unanalyzed `je m'appelle`/`tu t'appelles` phrase
  pair (grepped `s'appel|se lever|se lève|se couch|se lav|se douch|se
  réveill` across m1–m25, zero new hits). Still a verb-machinery gap
  (m24's own §1.3 re-classification stands), not attempted here.
- **d'-elision fix** — `getFrRealFormLexicon()` still omits `"d"` (§0.3);
  m26 does not depend on it (every new noun is consonant-onset or takes
  the already-licensed `l'` elision, never `d'`).

## 5. Homophone / liaison risks

- **`eau` vs `au` — a genuine short-token risk, the largest existing
  false-positive category course-wide.** `eau` [o] and `au` (the already-
  taught à+le contraction, `FR_FUNCTION_WORDS`, used in nearly every
  module since m4 — "au cinéma," "au musée") are a TRUE homophone. `eau`
  is a 2-normalized-char token after `frTokens`, squarely inside the
  short-token class `frSpeechMinimalPairs.test.ts`'s course-wide census
  already flags as the LARGEST category (27 of 60 hits,
  `docs/fr-speech-minimal-pairs-2026-09-10.md`). **`eau` never appears as
  an isolated, bare-word graded target anywhere in this module** — always
  embedded inside `"j'aime l'eau"` or a longer recombination (mirrors
  m25's own `qui`-embedding discipline and m24's `jus`-is-3-chars
  finding). A drafting agent must not author a bare `"eau"` speaking
  target or MCQ option under any circumstance.
- **`lait` vs `les` — a plausible but weaker near-pair, flagged not
  eliminated.** `lait` [lɛ] and `les` [le] (already-taught plural
  article) differ by vowel quality that a text-based ASR transcript may
  or may not preserve depending on the recognizer's own normalization —
  genuinely uncertain, not measured here (no bespoke probe was run, §6).
  Treated the same way m25 treated `qui`/`oui`: not currently exercised
  as a same-lesson sibling pair by this brief's own design, flagged as
  the concrete instance of the risk class for the drafting agent to keep
  clear of, not a generic warning.
- **No true homophone pair among the 8 new nouns themselves** — unlike
  m16's `allé`/`allée` or m24's `fermé`/`fermée`, none of this module's
  nouns is a gender/agreement PAIR (they are 8 independent nouns, not 4
  masculine/feminine forms of the same word), so no `homophoneKey` wiring
  is needed anywhere in this module — a genuinely lower speech-risk
  module than m16/m24's own adjective-pair beats.
- **No elision or liaison exposure beyond the already-licensed `l'eau`** —
  every other new noun is consonant-onset; `il y a un/une [room]` and
  `il n'y a pas de [staple]` are both fixed, already-proven frames with
  no new moving agreement part. This module introduces ZERO new `d'`
  exposure by design (mirrors m22/m24/m25's own "no new elision exposure
  by design" discipline).
