## Findings and what was done

### 1. m30 MAJOR — けっか as the thing looked up in a dictionary — FIXED
`けっかを じしょで しらべてみた` made けっか the object of a dictionary lookup —
backward: a dictionary answers ことば/いみ, けっか is the OUTPUT of a lookup.
QA saw it in review-1 (s-8 and the dialogue opener); the same collocation was
authored in the teaching lesson too (rule example, one beat, the te-miru cloze,
the L4 challenge — QA's scope couldn't see those). All six sites rewritten in
`ir/m30.ir.yaml` to ことば as the looked-up thing; the dialogue opener is now
「この ことばの いみは わかった？」, mirroring the module's own L4 line
「この ことばの いみは？」. けっか keeps its natural uses (けっかが わからない
から せんせいに きいてみる, recognition steps). 5 new TTS clips generated
(Nanami/edge, 0 failed), staged in `tts-publish/ja/`, manifest updated
(12325 → 12330 derivable).

### 2. m30 MAJOR — dialogue opener taught the same collocation — FIXED
Same root as finding 1; covered by the rewrite above.

### 3. m30 MINOR — noun-target MCQs pad with verb て-form distractors — NOT an IR fix
review-1/review-3/challenge `fill` steps offer て-form chunks as distractors
for noun targets (worst: challenge-fill-1, 2 of 3 distractors eliminable by
part-of-speech shape alone). These are compiler-generated filler steps, so the
fix belongs in the generator's distractor pool (POS-shape matching), not in any
module's IR. Recorded in the review queue with the concrete examples.

### 4. m32 MAJOR — review cloze grades なったら flatly wrong while the course concedes it is real — FIXED
`ふゆに ___、ゆきが ふる。` (options なると/なったと/なったら). The module's own
teaching card (ir/m32.ir.yaml:480) says "なったら IS real Japanese, but it makes
the weaker claim". The review cloze's explanation never argued why なったら
loses. First attempt (swap なったら for the pure form-error なるたら) was
rejected by m32's own guards — no dictionary+たら surface may exist even as an
option, and vocab provenance tracks option surfaces. Kept the options and gave
the cloze the teaching card's honest argument instead: なったら is real but
makes the weaker one-time claim; snow-every-winter is the law-like result と
states.

### 5. m32 MAJOR — challenge translate rejects the same defensible なったら — FIXED
Free-typed translate with no MCQ safety net. Added
`alsoAccept: ["ふゆに なったら ゆきが ふるから、くるまで いかない つもりだ。"]`
— the compiler's variant expander fans it out. Verified present in the compiled
step's acceptedAnswers.

### 6. m33 MAJOR — the one cloze without its pair-mate on offer — FIXED
Challenge cloze `みせの まえに くるまを ___ ください。` offered けして/しめて/とめて —
three transitive verbs — while its explanation claimed "the particle demands
the verb". Every other cloze in the module offers the answer's pair-mate.
Swapped けして → とまって. Root cause of the escape: `m33-neo.test.ts`'s
"pair partner always on offer" check keyed PARTNER on dictionary forms, so the
て-form answer とめて (and the resultative lesson's あいて/きえて) silently
skipped the lookup. The map now carries the て-form rows and unmapped answers
FAIL the test instead of skipping.

### 7. m33 MINOR class — explanations overclaim "the particle forces the verb" — FIXED, full sweep
QA flagged 5 review/challenge clozes where the third (non-pair) option shares
the shown particle and forms a natural sentence, yet the explanation claims the
particle alone forces the answer. A mechanical sweep of all 36 clozes found the
same class in the teaching lessons QA couldn't see: 17 sites total. Each now
carries one closing clause dismissing the third option by meaning
("しめる takes を too — the English says open, not close"). JA text untouched;
EN-only, so no TTS impact.
