# ES IR authoring rules

## Hard rule: dialogue_sim NPC lines

`esSurfaces()` has no `dialogue_sim` case, so NPC lines (`kana`/`audioText`) are invisible to the usual vocab/bar scans — an unregistered word in dialogue_sim shipped unnoticed by any scan, caught only by human review (a3580612, 0be21768, and the gate commit that landed after them). Every NPC line, not just graded replies, must resolve to atoms registered by that lesson's module — machine-enforced by `es-quality.test.ts` → describe("ES quality — dialogue_sim content resolves to registered atoms"). Exception: an NPC line (never a reply) may intentionally run ahead of taught vocabulary as an exposure device — the established "rapido"/"despacio" pattern — but only when rescued by a registered fixed phrase (e.g. "no entiendo"); replies stay bound to that lesson's module. Before shipping a sim, check its new NPC lines against the atom registry the same way you'd check a reply.

**Agreement is not a new atom.** A registered multi-word atom's final noun inflects for the sentence's number/gender exactly like a bare registered noun does — use whatever inflection real Spanish grammar requires; don't force a phrase atom's exact registered surface onto a sentence whose subject disagrees with it. «de niño» (m22, "as a child") is a noun phrase: a plural subject takes «de niños» («Sam y Luis vivían en México de niños», «¿Cómo eran ustedes de niños?»), and an explicitly all-female plural subject takes «de niñas» — the same regular -o/-a gender swap and regular plural the atom registry's own comment on "de niño" already documents, not a separate atom to register per form. `es-quality.test.ts`'s check (1) accepts any REGULAR plural/gender inflection of a phrase atom's final word (via the same `getEsPluralCanon`/`getEsGenderCanon`-style regular-inflection rule check (2) already applies to single-word nouns/adjectives course-wide) and rejects only a fabricated or irregular bend. Three prior rounds (a3580612, 0be21768, and the gate commit right after them) mechanically forced «de niño» onto plural-subject sentences to satisfy an earlier, stricter version of this gate that didn't yet model agreement — that produced ungrammatical Spanish and was the actual mistake; the gate was fixed, not the grammar.
