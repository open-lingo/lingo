## 0. What m40 teaches (from spine-n4.md, corrected below)

- **Morphology by class**, all three: godan (naiStem + れる: よむ → よまれる),
  ichidan (drop る, + られる: たべる → たべられる), irregular (くる → こられる,
  する → される).
- **⚠ THE CENTRAL PEDAGOGICAL CLAIM, verified against the live engine
  (2026-09-10):** for ichidan verbs and くる, the passive form is **BYTE-
  IDENTICAL** to the potential form the course has owned since m24. This is
  not spine rhetoric — `src/features/languages/ja/conjugationEngine.ts`
  computes both from the exact same stem formula (`naiStem + られる` /
  `s + られる`) and documents it explicitly in the `Stems.passive` JSDoc:
  *"Ichidan's naiStem + られる is BYTE-IDENTICAL to potential for every
  ichidan verb and for くる — that's not a bug, it's genuine Japanese."*
  `formationDistractors.ts` already relies on this for MCQ distractor
  generation (never hand-crafts "the other reading" as a decoy; natural
  dedupe handles it). **Frame this as the spine directs: a KNOWN SHAPE, new
  FRAME** — X が Y に V-られる — not a fresh conjugation to memorize. This is
  also pedagogy-doc rule 5 in miniature ("helpers, not conjugation
  paradigms" — られる is one attachment with two jobs, disambiguated by
  context, not two grammar points).
- **Direct passive, production.** X が Y に V-られる: 先生に ほめられた.
- **Possessed-object pattern, production — the everyday one.** さいふを
  ぬすまれた (lit. "as-for-me, wallet got stolen [on me]" — X が [Yの Z]を
  V-られる with the possessor as が-subject). This is the pattern learners
  actually need (my wallet got stolen, my foot got stepped on), distinct
  from the third-party "suffering passive" below.
- **によって/で creation and cause passive — RECOGNITION only** (この えは
  ゴッホによって かかれた). Spine names this explicitly as recognition, not
  production.
- **Suffering/adversity passive (迷惑の受身) — RECOGNITION only, production
  DEFERRED TO N3** (あめに ふられた — "got rained on", an intransitive verb
  taking a victim-subject with no direct-object equivalent in English at
  all). Do not build or cloze-grade this pattern; the spine's own words are
  "production deferred to N3."
- **Forward flag only:** honorific 尊敬語 use of the identical られる form
  lands at n4-20. Mention it exists (so the learner isn't blindsided later),
  do not teach it.
- **⟳ deepen of m24's potential system** — this is a BACKWARD spiral
  (m24 already exists, its content is compiled and can be pulled into
  review pools).
- **spiralWith: n4-16** (causative さ せる, spine m45) — this is a FORWARD
  spiral. n4-16 does not exist yet. **Unlike m39's spiralWith: m16 (backward,
  realized concretely in m39's own review lessons), m40 cannot pull review
  content from n4-16 — there is nothing to pull.** Realize this only as a
  forward-flag note in m40's `notes:` block and optionally a one-line
  closing-lesson mention ("this same shape comes back to mean *make/let*
  later") — no graded content, no review-pool dependency. n4-16's own future
  brief is where the backward half of this pairing actually gets built.

**why (spine's own words):** "After transitivity I, because passive is an
operation on a 他動詞 and the が/を diagnostic is the prerequisite skill.
Sixteen modules after potential, which is what makes the shared shape
teachable rather than confusing: it is presented as a KNOWN form with a new
particle frame — the one presentation that turns a collision into a saving.
Teaching passive before potential (as most textbooks are forced to) throws
that saving away."

## 4. Engineering prerequisite this module surfaces (not a blocker, but do it)

**Verified 2026-09-10 against the live repo — this is the single most
load-bearing finding in this brief, settling brief-request point (c):**

- `src/features/languages/ja/conjugationEngine.ts`'s `ChainForm` type
  **already includes `"passive"`** (marked `// UNTAUGHT` in the type
  comment) and `conjugateVerb(dictionary, group, "passive")` **already
  computes the correct form for all three verb classes** — the stem tables
  (`stemsOf()`) have carried working `passive` cells since before this
  module was dispatched. **The author does not need to hand-derive any
  passive form** — the engine is ground truth, exactly as it already is for
  every other formation point.
- **What does NOT exist yet:** a `passive` entry in `TRANSFORM_RULESETS`
  (`src/features/languages/ja/conjugation/transformRulesets.ts`). That file
  currently has exactly 5 entries: `nai`, `masu`, `te`, `volitional`, `ba`.
  Neither `potential` nor `passive` has ever had one.
- **inv 49 (`docs/authoring-invariants-pinned.md`) names passive られる
  explicitly as a formation point** that "ships with a rule table AND a
  transform card, or it does not ship" — and inv 49 is pinned dated
  2026-08-14, which is AFTER m24 (potential) was authored and BEFORE m30
  was authored (inv 49's own text: "establishing this before m30 is
  authored costs a paragraph"). **m24 predates inv 49 and is grandfathered
  — it is not the model to copy.** m24 taught every potential form as its
  own full `kind: verb` courseAtoms lemma row (のめる, たべられる, みられる,
  こられる, はなせる, あるける, できる — all real, live, SRS-tracked atoms
  today) via `kind: rule` grammar cards, never touching
  `conjugation_transform`/`TRANSFORM_RULESETS` at all.
- **`src/features/languages/ja/__tests__/irAtomRegistration.test.ts`
  (the "reverse arrow" ratchet, added after m24) explicitly forbids m24's
  approach going forward:** courseAtoms registration is **LEMMAS ONLY**.
  Its own doctrine comment: *"a word the IR declares as a NEW LEMMA must be
  registered. A form derived from one must not be"* — inflected forms are
  tagged `kind: "verb-form"` + `derivedFrom: <base>` in the IR and get NO
  courseAtoms row (m38's なくす/なくして pair is the shipped example).
- **Decision for this module (stated, not deferred):** m40 should use
  `conjugation_transform` cards against a newly-authored `passive` entry in
  `TRANSFORM_RULESETS`, NOT the m24-style per-form-lemma approach. Three
  independent reasons converge: (1) inv 49 requires it for a formation
  point named this explicitly; (2) pedagogy-doc rule 5 says formations are
  the Conjugation Trainer's job, lessons reinforce combinations — treating
  every passive surface as its own vocabulary word is the paradigm-not-
  helper framing the pedagogy doc forbids; (3) irAtomRegistration.test.ts's
  LEMMAS-only doctrine post-dates and effectively supersedes m24's pattern.
  **This means the author must add a `passive` `TransformRuleset` (3 rows:
  ichidan/godan/irregular, per the existing "ruleset shape" test's
  requirement) plus `RULESET_ALTERNATES["passive"]` leak-mask rows for any
  canonical example that doubles as a drilled base** — this is real,
  non-trivial engineering work inside `transformRulesets.ts`, budget time
  for it. `transformRulesets.test.ts`'s ratchets (leak-check, alternate-
  coverage, class-coverage) will hold the new entry to the same bar as
  `te`/`ba`/`volitional` automatically once it exists — they do not need
  their own bespoke edits, just a correct `passive` entry to check against.
