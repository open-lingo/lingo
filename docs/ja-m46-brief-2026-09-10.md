## §6 Explicitly rejected approaches

1. **Registering a fresh `うち` atom for `うちに` instead of riding the existing m16 possessive/
   deictic atom.** Rejected: this is the same lexeme's sense range widening (etymologically one word,
   "the space/domain of X" → "within X's state"), not two competing homographs fighting over one kana
   key the way m44's みたい was. Riding it as a rule-card-only reuse mirrors m44's own precedent for
   ようだ/みたい/のように/のような exactly.
2. **Registering a fresh bare `ところ` noun instead of cashing in the existing future-tagged stub.**
   Rejected: exact mirror of m45's そうじする decision — the stub already exists, is the spine's
   intended headword, `ところだ` IS built on this same lemma, and a fresh atom would risk a
   greedy-longest-match collision the moment ところ appears unspaced before だ/に in a するところだ-
   style sentence. Cash-in (with the dead-pointer fix, §1b) is strictly better and has direct
   precedent (m43 ぜったい, m45 そうじする).
3. **Block-teaching all four timing frames (間に/うちに/ところだ/たばかり) consecutively in lessons
   1-4 for "clean parallelism."** Rejected: violates interleave-dont-block-teach doctrine. The
   spine's own "consolidation module... one mechanism spent six ways" framing argues FOR interleaving
   distinct nominal hosts with unrelated breaks, not for stacking them — quantity-drip (L5) and the
   compound-verb trio (L6) are placed as genuine unrelated content between the frame pairs (L1/L3 vs
   L7), and the two ばかり surfaces (L5 quantity, L7 temporal) are separated by a full lesson so
   neither primes the other.
4. **Teaching quantity-ばかり and temporal-たばかり in the same lesson as an explicit "same kana, two
   grammars" discrimination card up front.** Rejected as the DEFAULT (though the eventual L7
   antiPattern card does perform this discrimination once both have independently debuted) — leading
   with the discrimination before either sense is independently secure risks exactly the kind of
   premature-reassembly fragmentation m44 warned against for its own evidential family; let each
   surface land on its own footing first (L5, L7), then contrast.
5. **Skipping つづく since the transitive つづける already covers "continue."** Rejected as the
   default: spine lists it explicitly under "prefer," and teaching the transitivity pair together
   deepens m33/m41's transitivity-family noticing (this module's own `spiralWith: n4-07` neighborhood)
   — higher pedagogical value than a convenience cut. Left as an author's-judgment flag for the L6
   draft, not a hard requirement.
6. **Hand-registering every reachable conjugated form of はじめる/つづける/おわる "just in case," now
   that all three are confirmed absent from `VERB_ENTRIES`.** Rejected: mirrors m45's own §2 budget
   discipline ("2-4 extra newAtoms for risk verbs," not "register everything reachable") — only
   hand-register the compound/conjugated surfaces actually drilled in an authored beat.
7. **Falling back to N3 spine reasoning.** Rejected: the N4 spine is not exhausted — m46 is n4-17 of
   51 mapped modules, directly following m45 (n4-16) with no gap in `docs/spine-n4.md`.
