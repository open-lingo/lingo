# Lingo Custom Art

SVG icons authored for Lingo where Google Noto Emoji has no matching glyph
or where the existing Noto glyph fails a usability audit. Style is
Noto-adjacent: flat fill, soft dark outline (~2px at 128px viewBox),
saturated primary colors, rounded geometry, transparent background.
